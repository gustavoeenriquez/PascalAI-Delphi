Bibliotecas de terceros incluidas / Third-party libraries included
==================================================================

PascalAI no usa FireDAC. Para hablar con PostgreSQL y con MySQL/MariaDB carga, al abrir la primera base,
estas bibliotecas cliente. Van en Win32\dll y Win64\dll: cópialas junto al .exe de tu aplicación (con la
carpeta plugin\), o deja su carpeta en el PATH. SQLite va dentro de PascalAI: no necesita nada.

PascalAI does not use FireDAC. To talk to PostgreSQL and MySQL/MariaDB it loads these client libraries when
the first database is opened. They are in Win32\dll and Win64\dll: copy them next to your application's .exe
(with the plugin\ folder), or put their folder on the PATH. SQLite is built into PascalAI.

Variables de entorno / Environment variables:
  PAI_PG_LIB      otra libpq.dll / another libpq.dll
  PAI_MYSQL_LIB   otra libmariadb.dll o libmysql.dll / another libmariadb.dll or libmysql.dll
